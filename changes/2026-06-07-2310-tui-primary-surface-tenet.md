# TUI Primary Surface Tenet

## Why

Steno has daemon, CLI, MCP, and settings surfaces, but the TUI is the primary
product experience. New capabilities should not become hidden power-user paths
that only exist outside the TUI.

## How

- Added a product tenet to `CLAUDE.md`: all user-visible functionality must be
  discoverable, triggerable, inspectable, or configurable from the TUI.
- Clarified that CLI and MCP paths complement the TUI rather than replacing it.

## Testing

- Documentation-only change; no runtime tests.
